# NIRVANA - Ethical AI & Responsible Use Guidelines

## 1. Core Principle: Human-in-the-Loop Decision Support
NIRVANA is an advisory analytical tool designed to enhance governance transparency and optimize resource allocation.
**It is not an automated judicial engine or criminal investigator.**

## 2. Strict Terminology Guidelines
- **NEVER use:** "Fraud detected", "Corrupt contractor", "Embezzlement proven".
- **ALWAYS use:**
  - *"Potential anomaly detected"*
  - *"Potential irregularity"*
  - *"High-risk pattern"*
  - *"Verification recommended"*
  - *"Data divergence noted"*

## 3. Mandatory Advisory Notice
Every dashboard screen, generated report, and prediction payload must visibly present:
> **"AI-generated risk indicators are decision-support signals and do not constitute proof of fraud, corruption, or wrongdoing."**

## 4. Bias, Fairness & Regional Nuance
- High construction costs in remote, mountainous, or island regions (e.g. Ladakh, Northeast States, Andaman & Nicobar) must not be mechanically classified as anomalies without regional cost-index calibration.
- Project types must never be compared across disjoint classes (e.g. comparing the per-unit cost of a multi-specialty hospital building against a rural gravel pathway).
