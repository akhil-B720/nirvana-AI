NIRVANA Platform Purpose & Mission

SIH Problem ID: SIH26102 | Category: Software

NIRVANA is an AI-powered decision-support platform engineered to identify statistical anomalies, expenditure inefficiencies, execution delays, and reality divergences in the implementation of the Member of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme (MPLADS). By fusing historical expenditure disclosures with spatial similarity graphs and field verification workflows, NIRVANA equips vigilance officers with empirical evidence to prioritize on-site physical audits.

Statutory Legal Notice & Ethical AI Boundary

All analytical risk indicators, anomaly rankings, and cluster assignments produced by the NIRVANA platform are mathematical decision-support estimations designed to assist oversight officials in optimizing audit resources. An elevated analytical risk score does NOT constitute legal evidence, judicial proof, or administrative determination of corruption, misallocation, or fraudulent wrongdoing. All determinations require corroboration through certified human physical inspection.
